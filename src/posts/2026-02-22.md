# Christianity

## Translations
For the longest time I looked for 'the best translation' possible of the Bible, I did all of this research and I came to a firm conclusion, there is no one best translation and that instead of vainly looking for this best translation one should use more then a singular translation of the Bible. The reason for using more then one translation of the Bible is to prevent translator bias and translator mistakes, this allows us as Christians to get a firmer image of what the original languages that form the holy scriptures try to convey to us. However if I did how to choose the best translation of the Bible it would be an interlinear Bible with English aids, so you are able to see the original language with the English equivalent.

### Top three translations
- KJV
- NASB
- RSV
- BSB

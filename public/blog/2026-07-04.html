College

At my college I am taking a T-level course in computing. I have mixed feelings about my course, this is because it feels as if a lot of what we are being taught is out of date, and there are also some bits and pieces which have just been misinformation. For example, one of my tutors said that UNIX owns Linux and Linux owns Android, which even if it were a simplification is stupid. The GNU/Linux operating system is not even based on UNIX, no code from UNIX to my knowledge is found within the GNU/Linux operating system, this is because GNU/Linux is UNIX-like meaning that it is designed to act like UNIX. Another example of something which I disliked was when they were teaching us about phishing, and they gave us the classical education on the matter, the basics. Look for spelling mistakes, check links, all of that. However, in the year of our Lord 2026 we have Ai and spoofing, which can make phishing emails look rather real. On my work placement I received a better education on phishing emails, this is because in the training which I was given to do they had a white hack hacker showing how phishing works and it was rather interesting. I can go on about issues I have with the course.

Although I may have many parts of the course which I dislike, there are parts which I like. For example we as students were allowed to take apart computers and put them back together. When me and my friends did this we speed ran each other, this make putting the computers together and learning how the computers work very fun. We also for some reason learnt about windows servers. From what I did of the window server OS, Linux was way easier. Why would one require a GUI to use a server, sure maybe for visualizing data. But for everything, it is silly. Another part of this course which I like is the class is very small, with only nine students, myself included. This leads to the class being more quiet and I have found that I am most likely the loudest student which feels weird because I am not the most outspoken person.

I do feel unmotivated in my studies and I do think that in a way this course may have killed my love for tech. My course makes me come in daily to College which is fine, but some days it is nine to five and I just feel tired and miserable by the time I am home. However, I am guessing if you are reading this and have a job you feel the same. But lately within the last month or so my love for technology has been revived and as you will soon read I have many plans.
